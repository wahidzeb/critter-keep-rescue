About Critter Keep Rescue

Critter Keep Rescue mixes gentle sneaking with critter collecting. Cheeky raccoon bandits have borrowed your critters, and you tiptoe through hedge mazes to bring them home, then hatch eggs and merge critters in your cosy keep.

Each bandit has a yellow cone showing what he can see. There are six biomes to explore, and stars depend on how many times you were caught. You always finish the level in the end, so younger players never get stuck.

3 quick tips

  1. Stay out of the yellow vision cones and use bushes. You are hidden while standing or walking in a bush.
  2. Dashing through a bush does not hide you, so walk through it instead.
  3. Never getting caught gives 3 stars; 1 or 2 catches gives 2 stars.

How to play

🏡 Your Critter Keep: cute critters live on your stumps and make coins all the time, even when you're away!

🦝 Sneaky raccoon bandits try to borrow your critters. Tap a bandit to shoo it away. Build fences, a bell and a guard puppy in Defend.

🌳 Rescue levels: drag anywhere to move and tap DASH to zoom. Stay out of the bandits' yellow look-out beams and hide in bushes.

🔑 Grab keys for locked cages, free the critters and lead them back to HOME. Getting caught just sends you back to start!

🥚 Hatch eggs for new critters. Merge two of the same critter to get a rarer one!
